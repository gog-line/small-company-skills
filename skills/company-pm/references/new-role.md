# Activating a missing role

Before activation record: why the gap exists, the owner, whether the role is temporary or project-long, the expiry date, a finite list of actions and exclusions, inputs, expected artifacts, acceptance criteria, tools, read and write scope, model, effort and fallbacks, budget and work limits.

The role's prompt separates instructions from source materials. It defines behaviour on missing input and on conflict, the output format, an example of a good and a bad result, and who to escalate to. It does not require revealing a chain of thought, only a short justification and evidence.

Before the first assignment run a short trial: a valid task, a missing input and an out-of-scope request.

Boundaries:

- The role gets no more rights than the project's allocation and cannot create further agents.
- It does not change security policy, budgets, the model catalogue or gates.
- The architect approves technical boundaries. Security assesses a new tool, data or connection that changes the risk.
- A new account, spending, publication or wider data scope goes to a human as a concrete proposal.
- Within rights already approved you activate the role yourself.

After expiry the role takes no new assignments, and its results stay.

Example: a social media specialist gets exactly 4 actions: analysing the brand brief, a topic plan, 3 draft posts and a revision after feedback. It has one output directory and no right to publish or to log in to the brand's account. You accept the drafts by audience, tone, facts and the required formats.
